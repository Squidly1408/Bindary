Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Bindary ("Bindary", "we", "us") is operated by Lucas Peter-Ernest Newman, a sole trader based in Australia. This policy explains what information Bindary collects when you use the app at bindary-books.web.app, why it's collected, and the choices you have about it.

Businesses with an annual turnover under AUD $3 million are generally exempt from the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and Bindary currently falls under that exemption. This policy is provided anyway, as a matter of good practice and transparency about how your data is actually handled.

1. Information Bindary collects

Account information. When you create an account, Bindary (via Firebase Authentication) collects the email address and password you sign up with, and an optional display name you can set later. Your password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication — Bindary never sees or stores it in plain text.

Content you create. The tasks you add, and the poems/books Bindary generates when you complete them, are stored under your account so your library persists across sessions and devices.

Settings. Small preferences you configure, like agenda lead-time days, are stored the same way.

Bindary does not collect your name, address, or phone number, and does not ask for any information beyond what's listed above.

2. How your information is used

  • To create and secure your account, and keep your tasks/library in sync.
  • To respond if you contact Bindary for support.
  • To keep the service working reliably and to detect abuse.

Your task and poem content is never sold, and is never used to train any model.

3. Where your information is stored

Account data, tasks, and library content are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Firebase may process and store data on servers outside Australia. Access to your data is restricted by Firestore security rules to your own signed-in account — see Firebase's own privacy documentation for how Google handles data on its infrastructure.

4. Analytics

Bindary is free and shows no ads. It loads a Google Ads conversion tag site-wide to measure the performance of its own ad campaigns — it records that a visit or sign-up happened, not what you do inside the app. You can control how Google personalizes ads shown to you at adssettings.google.com, and read Google's own practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Cookies and local storage

Bindary keeps you signed in between visits using your browser's local storage (Firebase Auth's persistence). The Google Ads tag sets its own cookies as described above. Bindary does not use any first-party analytics or tracking cookies of its own.

6. Children's privacy

Bindary is not directed at children and is not knowingly used by anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us using the details below and it will be removed.

7. Your rights and choices

You can update your username, email, and password at any time from Settings → Account inside the app. To request a copy of your data, ask that it be corrected, or ask for your account and all associated data to be deleted, email the address below — Bindary doesn't currently have automated self-service export or deletion tools, so these requests are handled manually, and will normally be actioned within 30 days.

8. Security

Access to your data is enforced by Firestore security rules tied to your signed-in account. No method of storing or transmitting data online is perfectly secure, and Bindary can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be flagged inside the app where practical.
