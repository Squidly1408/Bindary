Privacy Policy

Bindary - Last updated 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bindary, a web app that turns your completed tasks into poems and stores them in a private library, collects, how it's used, and your choices. Bindary is an independent project operated by Lucas Newman (robotduck961@gmail.com). This is a plain-language policy for a small independent project, not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

  • Account information (email address, authentication identifiers) via Firebase Authentication.
  • The tasks and poems you create, stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Payment information if you subscribe to a paid tier - card details are handled entirely by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Standard web analytics and advertising identifiers via Google Search Console, Google Analytics/Ads, and Google AdSense (cookies and similar technologies), used to understand site usage and show ads.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) - authentication and data storage (Firestore)
  • Stripe - payment processing for paid subscriptions
  • Google AdSense / Google Ads / Search Console - advertising and site-usage analytics

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate, maintain, and improve Bindary - for example, to let you sign in, save your data, process payments you've requested, or show the app's live status. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at robotduck961@gmail.com. If Bindary offers an account, you can typically delete your own data from within the app; otherwise, email us and we'll action your request as soon as reasonably possible.

Data retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by our service providers' own retention settings (e.g. Firebase). We take reasonable steps to protect data but, as with any online service, we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Bindary is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of Bindary after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email robotduck961@gmail.com.
